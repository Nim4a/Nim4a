### picoSun2

**Rust · egui** — full-resolution photo viewer for Windows. RAW support, a per-folder cache that makes reopens near-instant, and a filmstrip of the whole folder.

| RAW demosaic (26MP ARW) | Filmstrip fill | VRAM ceiling |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **4s** | **0.35s** | **285MB** |

[Repository](https://github.com/Nim4a/picoSun2) · [Download .exe](https://github.com/Nim4a/picoSun2/releases/latest)

**Rust · egui · Win32** — a dark, tabbed notepad with line numbers and syntax highlighting. Persian text renders correctly through the native RichEdit control, not a webview.
